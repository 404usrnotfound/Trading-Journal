# Asset-class extensibility audit

Phase 11 audits the existing financial implementation against the [product specification](../Trading_Journal_Codex_Prompt.md), [domain model](domain-model.md), [architecture](architecture.md) and [capability contracts](instrument-capabilities.md). It strengthens representative fixtures and financial-detail compatibility checks. It does not add native derivative or fixed-income accounting.

The operation matrix below describes implemented behavior. The full-product design matrix in [instrument capabilities](instrument-capabilities.md) remains a delivery obligation. Phase 11 test execution and regression outcomes belong in [Phase 11 validation](phase-11-validation.md); a fixture's existence alone is not evidence that it passed.

## Implemented operation matrix

“Native” means the existing validated ordinary-linear calculation path, within its metadata and source-input gates. “Recorded” means accepted source facts and actual cash postings, with provenance; it does not establish automatic instrument-specific economics. An unavailable monetary result remains null with reasons, including `unsupported_inventory_module`.

Other required families can use the generic manual instrument catalog, explicit cash and journal/import evidence, subject to the same limits. [Advanced financial entry](advanced-financial-entry.md) now exposes the supported explicit source commands in the UI. [Reviewed supplied values](supplied-values.md) can establish an explicitly selected residual position value or compatible campaign net result, with source, quantity, revision, freshness and completeness checks; they do not enable native instrument formulas. Full specialized lifecycle workflows and the specification's advanced automatic acceptance scenarios remain separate capability gates.

The previous validated ordinary-linear behavior and its regression evidence are documented in [core ledger](core-ledger.md), [financial metrics](financial-metrics.md), [portfolio accounting](portfolio-accounting.md) and the phase validation reports. Newly added Phase 11 examples recheck specific representative conventions; they do not promote a whole family to native accounting.

## Gates that prevent a universal stock formula

The server permits `capability: "native-linear"` only for these exact pairs:

- `stock` / `ordinary-share`.
- `etf` / `ordinary-etf`.
- `crypto_spot` / `non-rebasing-spot`.

Each requires known quantity/price increments, multiplier `"1"`, equal known quote and settlement currency, `metadata.priceConvention: "unit-price"` and `metadata.settlementStyle: "delivered"`. Stocks/ETFs require quantity unit `share`; spot crypto additionally requires `metadata.rebasing: false`. Automatic inventory also requires a netted account. A family label, broker description or supplied multiplier does not grant permission to derive principal/P&L.

For stock/ETF shorts, the exact pinned version must also declare `shortConvention: "borrowed-delivery"` and `carryCostPolicy: "explicit-source-charges"`. Actual borrowing costs still require source charges. These declarations do not supply broker borrow availability or buying power. Crypto spot does not inherit this short convention.

The relevant boundaries are `validateNativeInstrumentMetadata` / `assertNativeInstrumentMetadata` in [reference-data.ts](../packages/server/src/financial/reference-data.ts), execution cash dispatch in [ledger.ts](../packages/server/src/financial/ledger.ts), and the supported-subtype guard in [inventory.ts](../packages/domain/src/inventory.ts). The pure kernel consumes an explicit capability/context from the trusted application layer; a manually constructed kernel input is not a public capability grant.

## Actual cash, quantity and missing economics

`capability: "manual"` permits truthful nullable multiplier, increments, currencies and price. An actual cash effect still requires an explicit bucket, currency and decimal amount. A manual fill's `bookedPrincipal` with its currency is retained evidence; it does **not** automatically debit or credit cash. Supply `manualCashEffects` for the actual effect, or record another appropriately classified actual monetary command. Review source-effect identity to avoid recording the same settlement twice.

Generic cash arithmetic knows amounts and currencies, not how an option premium, futures settlement or bond dirty consideration should be derived. A source settlement can therefore update cash correctly while its instrument basis/P&L remains unsupported. A known manual quantity can be displayed independently of unavailable monetary results. Supplying a stock-like multiplier or mark does not turn those results into native calculations.

Manual and broker account valuation observations retain amount, stated definition, observation time and source. They are comparison evidence; the current engine does not select them as replacement account NAV or distribute them to holdings. The separate [supplied-component workflow](supplied-values.md) accepts only explicitly defined position residuals or campaign results and requires explicit report selection. Without compatible selected evidence, an unsupported open holding leaves NAV partial/unavailable with the known subtotal; recorded cash alone is not complete equity.

Delivered FX uses two actual currency legs and separately charged fees. Translation into another reporting currency does not create another conversion or cash posting. A currency-pair identity can describe a source record, but is not an additional owned security worth the acquired currency amount. This prevents counting USD cash and a EUR/USD pair holding for the same delivered exchange.

Campaign closure is separate from accounting capability. It requires actual assigned activity, exactly zero assigned quantity on every leg, no detected unresolved cash obligations and explicit `obligationEvidence`. A manual campaign can close with that evidence while monetary P&L and R remain unavailable. The application does not automatically infer that an option expired, a future settled or a bond redeemed from its metadata date.

## Findings addressed in this increment

The journal financial-detail path assumed that every multiplier was non-null, even though manual metadata intentionally permits unknown values. A truthful manual contract must remain readable without substituting multiplier `1` or enabling monetary calculations.

The journal also compared quantity unit, quote currency and multiplier while combining fills, without comparing the price convention. For example, a bond quote `0.98` per unit and a quote `98` percent of face are not directly comparable; their numerical average `49.49` has no defined financial meaning. Financial detail must compare the pinned economic conventions before combining prices. Incompatible execution units produce an explained unavailable result while source executions and known quantities remain visible. Analytics already checks these version conventions; journal detail must uphold the same distinction.

Compatible raw quoted-price averages also retain their actual convention. A unit-price quote can carry currency per quantity unit; a percentage-of-face quote is labeled `quote/percentage-of-face`, with no invented currency-per-face unit. A known raw average does not establish a native monetary basis or result.

Missing or oversized quote conventions leave the average unavailable with a bounded `quote/unknown` unit, so arbitrary manual metadata cannot break the detail response. The journal editor describes prices in the instrument's recorded quote convention rather than assuming every source quote is an amount of quote currency.

The accounting opening-capital path separately applied quantity × multiplier × mark without verifying an opening instrument's native valuation capability. For a manual future, two contracts at `5,000` with multiplier `50` could therefore invent `500,000` of contributed capital despite unavailable NAV. Opening valuation now requires the validated native convention, the effective netted-account setting and the appropriate short convention. Unsupported opening holdings preserve recorded quantity and cash; their capital, trading performance and FX effects remain unavailable rather than treating notional as value.

The regression fixtures cover nullable manual multipliers, changed price conventions and unsupported opening-capital valuation. No conversion factor or class-specific formula is invented to make an incompatible average or notional valuation available.

## Extensible metadata and immutable history

Common fields describe shared units and capability boundaries: quantity unit, nullable multiplier/increments, quote/settlement currency, effective time and capability. Class-specific terms stay in the instrument version's `metadata` JSON payload or, when a future validated operation needs relational invariants, a specialized domain structure.

| Class   | Examples of specialized versioned terms                                                                                           |
| ------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Crypto  | Chain/network, native-asset designation or contract address, rebasing and custody/representation convention.                      |
| Forex   | Base/quote roles, delivered versus rolling subtype, contract size, pip/tick convention, collateral and carry terms.               |
| Options | Underlying reference, put/call, strike, expiry, exercise style, settlement style, deliverable and adjustment evidence.            |
| Futures | Contract month/expiry, multiplier units, price/tick/tick-value convention, settlement/collateral and variation-margin definition. |
| Bonds   | Face-value unit, price scale, clean/dirty convention, accrued-interest evidence, coupon/day-count terms and maturity.             |

Recording these terms does not claim that all are validated or evaluated by a specialized handler. Current manual payload validation is generic JSON validation; there is no native options, futures or bonds metadata validator. The application does not require strike, coupon or expiry columns on every instrument.

Accepted executions and observations reference an exact immutable instrument version. Catalog publication appends a new version; old payloads remain readable. Ordinary updates cannot silently replace known multiplier, quantity or monetary units. Changing capability after executions/opening lots have used the instrument is blocked. New contract expiries, strikes or other economic identities should be separate instruments, not reuse an old identity to rewrite history. An explicitly reviewed transformation/event is required where inventory economics actually change.

## Adding a subtype safely

For manual recording, use a distinct stable `family`, `subtype` and `economicIdentity`; set `capability: "manual"`, state the quantity unit and known currency/convention facts, and preserve specialized terms/source evidence in metadata. Use explicit actual cash rather than inferring a purchase obligation. Existing [canonical imports](imports.md) can carry these financial DTOs through the shared command boundary; a broker parser neither grants native eligibility nor embeds accounting formulas.

Adding native accounting is a separate financial capability increment:

1. Define the exact economic subtype, dimensional units, source-price envelope, settlement/collateral behavior and supported operations. Obtain named contract/issuer/venue evidence where required.
2. Implement vetted server-side metadata validation and a specialized pure handler with a versioned calculation manifest. Keep premium, daily settlement, accrued interest and inverse payoff rules out of the ordinary delivered-share path.
3. Dispatch to that handler through the shared financial application boundary; preserve explicit manual fallback and explained missing/unsupported results. Broker adapters continue to normalize into shared commands.
4. Add independent numerical fixtures plus quantity/cash/cost conservation, immutable metadata, correction/replay, incompatible-input and real-PostgreSQL atomicity/authorization tests.
5. Add actual event/review/import flows and publish the operation-specific evidence and limits before changing the support matrix.

The current native selection is an explicit allowlist and shared dispatcher, not a public plug-in registry that can accept an arbitrary formula. Promoting a new subtype currently requires reviewed gate/dispatch code changes, but no broker-format change or irrelevant common instrument columns. A richer instrument-handler registry remains an architectural extension point; arbitrary uploaded/user JavaScript is never executed.

## Remaining limits and delivery gates

- **Signed source prices:** manual futures executions, opening entry quotes and price observations retain signed source values, including negative prices. They do not derive notional cash, basis, P&L or NAV. Native delivered execution/opening prices remain positive and native marks nonnegative. Order limit/stop price fields retain their separate positive-price validation envelope.
- **Advanced lifecycle events:** metadata can describe expiry, exercise, daily settlement and redemption, but there are no specialized automatic event/basis handlers for those cases. Explicit cash plus a contract quantity record is not a complete exercise, physical delivery or amortization model.
- **Supplied advanced values:** explicitly selected [reviewed components](supplied-values.md) can provide compatible residual position equity or campaign net results. They do not supply automatic acquisition basis, lifecycle settlement or trading/FX decomposition, and whole-account observations remain comparison evidence. The design's broader Level 2 workflows retain separate gates.
- **Currency/inventory representation:** the domain requires one representation of the same owned token/currency units. Generic independent catalog and cash records do not establish an automatic custody-representation conversion workflow; users must avoid claiming both as the same holding.
- **Source completeness:** unknown costs, basis, chronology, prices or FX retain their unavailable states. A closed manual campaign does not establish a complete result or native R.

Phase 11 leaves the approved full-product requirements intact. Options, futures and bonds are recordable within the stated source envelope, with explicit actual cash and honest unavailable results; their full accounting and the specification's advanced acceptance scenarios remain unresolved. There is no claim of comprehensive asset-class support or jurisdictional tax compliance.
