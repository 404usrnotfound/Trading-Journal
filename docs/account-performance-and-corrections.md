# Account returns and historical corrections

The Accounts screen now offers separate raw NAV and flow-adjusted account series, plus historical reversals for typed income, tax, expense and owned-transfer sources. These are independent of campaign P&L analytics.

## Correct an erroneous account fact

Editors and owners select a historical source in Accounts, enter a reason, review the effect and confirm. `GET /api/v1/workspaces/{workspaceId}/accounting/corrections` pages immutable sources (25 by default, at most 100); `POST .../corrections/{sourceId}/reverse` requires the source type, expected current financial revision, reason, CSRF proof and an idempotency key.

The command appends exact balanced negations at the original economic instants. Reversing income also reverses its linked withholding/tax sources that have not already been reversed. Reversing a transfer reverses its departure and any receipt together; a reversed transfer cannot later receive a receipt. Original records, actor evidence, audit and outbox history remain immutable. Existing PostgreSQL reversal/conservation and financial-command completeness checks still apply.

Pinned reports before the reversal retain their original results. Reports at the correction revision remove the erroneous historical cash, income/tax/expense classification, transfer transit and scope-boundary effect. Reversed financing no longer blocks campaign cost completeness. Retrying the same command identity returns its committed result; a different duplicate reversal or stale review conflicts.

To replace an incorrect source, reverse it, then record the reviewed corrected source with its original economic time and a description linking the original source. These are two explicit financial commands: the reversal can stand alone, and the interim reversed state remains visible until the replacement commits. This workflow must not be used to represent a genuine later refund; record that actual later event with its actual date instead. The UI freezes reviewed fields after an unknown response and retains the same command identity for confirmation retries.

## Account series and return definitions

`POST /api/v1/workspaces/{workspaceId}/accounting/performance` is an authorized read-only query using the accounting report scope, reporting currency, price/FX policy and pinned financial revision. Viewers may read it. Clients supply optional display checkpoint instants, never monetary balances. The server discovers capital-flow boundaries from actual ledger postings, owned cash transfers, opening inventory and holding transfers in the selected account scope; it includes every boundary automatically. Contributions and owned transfers crossing the selected scope are capital flows; transfers internal to the consolidated scope cancel.

At most 24 optional display checkpoints and 32 total closing/flow/sample instants are accepted per query. A larger required boundary population returns an explicit scope error; select a shorter period. Checkpoints are ordered with microsecond precision. The server values every checkpoint using the declared report price/FX selection and freshness policy. These are policy-based valuations, not interpolated prices. Results retain raw NAV, capital flow and the pinned source revision.

TWR uses post-flow NAV checkpoints. For each subperiod, its pre-flow ending NAV is `ending NAV − net capital flow`. The growth factor is that pre-flow NAV divided by the previous post-flow NAV. The unit index starts at 100; the period TWR is the product of growth factors minus one. Complete positive capital and valuation/flow evidence are required at every boundary. Nonpositive denominators, missing evidence or output overflow produce explained unavailability, never zero. Because current snapshots cannot address an intra-timestamp ordering key, a capital-flow instant coinciding with an income/cost/trade event makes TWR unavailable (`ambiguous_flow_ordering`); the engine does not guess which capital earned that result.

MWR solves the investor cash-flow equation using negative opening NAV, negative contributions/positive withdrawals at their exact instants, and positive ending NAV. ACT/365 uses actual nanosecond differences divided by 365 days. It needs complete opening/ending NAV and all dated capital flows; intermediate valuation gaps alone do not block MWR. A single sign change in the aggregated, ordered investor cash flows proves one positive discount-factor root. Patterns whose uniqueness is not established return unavailable rather than selecting an arbitrary root. The solver bisects annual log growth over `[-80, 80]` for up to 256 iterations and verifies an NPV residual no greater than `1e-24` of gross absolute cash flows. No bracket, ambiguous/no solution, failed convergence or numerical/output overflow is explicit. MWR is an annualized decimal ratio; TWR is a period decimal ratio. Neither sums campaign percentages or R multiples.

Drawdown uses the unit index relative to its previous peak, not raw NAV. Maximum drawdown is the most negative sampled drawdown. Recovery duration measures peak-to-recovery elapsed time at observed checkpoints; it is null when still unrecovered or unavailable. The charts and data tables explicitly state their sampling: they do not claim to detect an unobserved intraperiod maximum drawdown. A deposit cannot raise the unit index or recover an investment loss. Missing boundaries break the adjusted series, while available raw NAV remains visible.

## Regression evidence

`tests/unit/account-performance.test.ts` covers independently known annual returns, contributions, drawdown/recovery, missing evidence, nonpositive capital, ambiguous roots, supported solver bounds and arithmetic overflow. `tests/integration/portfolio-accounting-reports.test.ts` exercises real restricted PostgreSQL sources, complete ten-percent annual TWR/MWR, automatic flow discovery, revision pinning, missing FX, ambiguous same-instant timing, typed reversals and linked withholding/transfer conservation. `tests/integration/analytics-services.test.ts` checks that reversing typed financing restores campaign cost completeness only at the appropriate revision. `tests/ui/account-remediation.test.tsx` covers read-only viewers, retained reversal retries after a lost response and distinct pinned raw/adjusted charts.
