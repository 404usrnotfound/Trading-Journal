# Risk policies, assessment evidence and sizing

The **Risk & sizing** workspace provides immutable account/strategy policies, point-in-time monitoring and pre-trade planning. It does not place trades, prevent recording a real breach, change posted financial records, or replace original committed risk used by historical R.

Publish a policy against one account or one strategy identity. Its numbered versions contain an explicit effective instant, reason, typed limits, source priority and maximum observation age. Editing publishes another version. An empty limits list is allowed and imposes no invented numerical restriction. Publishing an empty replacement version retires that policy's restrictions from its effective instant while retaining history.

An assessment selects an account, an optional explicit strategy version, an economic instant at or before now and an optional proposed trade. It applies the latest effective version of **every** policy for that account and selected strategy. Strategy policies restrict the account used for that strategy; they do not assume a separately capitalized strategy portfolio. Account and strategy restrictions are both evaluated. For comparable maximum limits this implements the strictest bound; prohibited sets combine by union. Different currencies, populations and measures remain separate assessments. A looser strategy cannot erase an account breach.

Assessments retain actor/time, policy snapshots and references, evaluated operands and units, reasons, proposal evidence, calculation version and financial source revision. They are immutable records with a permanent authorized URL. New activity, prices, FX or policy publications require a new assessment; an old result is evidence for its captured context, not a live compliance badge. A shared financial-revision lock prevents concurrent financial commands from changing the source generation during capture. Risk publication uses separate command/audit history and never advances the financial revision. A first assessment of an empty workspace may establish its zero-revision state row without posting any financial event.

| Limit                  | Explicit definition and inputs                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Per-trade risk         | Proposed source/scenario amount translated to the configured currency; optionally divided by complete positive account equity. An amount, currency and method are required together. No original-risk commitment is fabricated.                                                       |
| Daily/weekly loss      | `max(0, -net_result)` within the configured IANA day or Monday-starting week. Choose realized activity or complete account change excluding capital flows; choose whether actual period income contributes.                                                                           |
| Concurrent positions   | Distinct nonzero actual holding books under the account convention; a proposed new instrument adds one book. A proposal in an already active instrument does not add a second campaign count. Unknown quantities remain unknown.                                                      |
| Concentration          | Absolute monetary exposure of a selected instrument, or the largest individual instrument exposure, divided by complete positive account equity. Currency and instrument population are explicit.                                                                                     |
| Exposure/leverage      | Sum of absolute instrument exposure, or absolute signed net account exposure; choose an absolute monetary cap or ratio to complete positive account equity. Long/short directions are preserved.                                                                                      |
| Overnight              | A current remaining lot or proposed holding interval crosses the configured local boundary hour. Proposals require intended exit evidence. Ambiguous/nonexistent DST boundaries, unsupported lot history and transferred lots without account-specific session evidence stay unknown. |
| Prohibited instruments | Union of reviewed instrument identity, asset-family and subtype sets applied to active holdings and the proposed instrument. No arbitrary expression or user JavaScript executes.                                                                                                     |

For the realized-activity loss measure, matched gross principal P&L is translated at realization and includes realized holding FX from acquisition to realization. Compatible pinned source rates take precedence, with explicit historical source/freshness selection otherwise. Actual fees, expenses and taxes are charged once on their period charge dates. Optional period income is added separately. The calculation does **not** begin with fee-net lot P&L and deduct fees again. A deposit, withdrawal, opening adjustment or owned-account transfer is not trading profit. Complete account-change loss uses the existing interval accounting bridge, including supported marked effects and capital-flow exclusion. Excluding income subtracts the separately identified period income from that result; actual taxes and costs remain charges.

Local periods use the policy timezone, independent of later workspace display preferences. The start is inclusive, implemented through the preceding PostgreSQL microsecond and the accounting engine's `(from,to]` interval. Unsupported/incomplete realization, missing acquisition chronology, historical FX, costs, boundary valuations or capital coverage prevents a passed financial limit. A ratio requires strictly positive complete equity. Short/custom/derivative notional is never substituted for supported monetary holding exposure or account NAV. Missing proposed risk or nonlinear exposure is unknown even if a source quantity is known.

Each result is `passed`, `breached` or `unknown`; an assessment without effective limits is `unconfigured`, not approved risk. An established breach takes precedence in the overall state even when another limit is unknown. Otherwise any unknown prevents an overall pass. Result details disclose the operands and evidence needed for review. These monitoring results inform action but never alter or reject an actual financial record.

## Planned size

Native sizing is enabled only for validated ordinary shares, ordinary ETFs and non-rebasing spot instruments in the supported netted delivered convention. Shorts additionally require the account's short permission and the instrument's explicit borrowed-delivery/carry-cost convention. Unknown multipliers, increments, quote/settlement conventions or unsupported nonlinear products require manual risk.

For direction `d` (long `+1`, short `-1`):

`distance = d × (entry − stop)` must be strictly positive.

`risk_per_unit = distance × multiplier × quote_to_risk_FX + estimated_cost_per_unit`

`quantity = floor((budget − fixed_costs) / risk_per_unit / quantity_increment) × quantity_increment`

`planned_risk = quantity × risk_per_unit + fixed_costs` for a positive quantity; a zero-quantity, zero-risk result creates no order when the budget cannot fund a positive increment. All authoritative arithmetic uses the isolated Decimal context, with downward increment rounding before the scale-36 output boundary. A risk-per-unit result below the representable decimal quantum is unavailable rather than zero. No capital, risk percentage, leverage or minimum trade size is assumed. Costs are explicitly entered in the risk currency; zero costs must be entered deliberately. Embedded spread/slippage is not charged again as actual accounting expense.

Different quote and risk currencies require a selected historical planning FX observation, with pair direction, age and supersession checked. Inverse pairs are handled explicitly. The result retains metadata/settings/FX references. It is a planned stop scenario, **not a guarantee of realized loss or broker margin availability**.

For complex instruments, choose **Explicit manual scenario risk**, enter the reviewed scenario/max-loss method, quantity and total initial risk including planned costs. The supplied amount stays visibly manual. A scenario exceeding the explicit budget is disclosed. It does not promote the instrument to native pricing or sizing and does not create or overwrite an original plan/R denominator. To assess a proposed trade, enter that sizing or manual scenario amount with its currency and method in the assessment form.

## Boundaries and validation

Policies, versions, commands and assessments use PostgreSQL persistence, same-workspace foreign keys, forced RLS and immutable-history triggers. Viewers can read policy history/stored assessments and use read-only sizing; editors/owners publish policy versions and capture assessments with mutation-session/CSRF checks and idempotency keys. Retries reuse the same command identity; changed content conflicts. Scope changes require a new policy identity. Policy and catalog history is paginated; reference searches run on the server, including historical instrument/strategy versions.

One definition contains at most 32 limits and 64 KiB. Capture accepts at most 16 applicable policies and 200 holding books, with a 1 MiB evidence/result budget. Evidence outside a calculation's supported capacity yields an explained unknown or bounded request rejection, never a partial pass. This is synchronous monitoring, not unattended real-time enforcement or a claim of unlimited reporting capacity. Strategy-specific portfolio accounting, issuer/category aggregation beyond explicit instrument/family/subtype eligibility, exchange holiday calendars, automated broker margin and advanced nonlinear scenario engines are not implied by these typed restrictions. Account-level limits and all required limit kinds remain usable with explicit scope and honest missing-evidence behavior.

Verification sources are `tests/unit/risk.test.ts`, `tests/integration/risk.test.ts` and `tests/ui/risk.test.tsx`. They include independent exact sizing arithmetic, all required limit kinds, account/strategy precedence, missing/stale evidence, period fee/deposit treatment, immutable effective versions, concurrent stale publication, persisted retry behavior, authorization/CSRF and the actual UI publication/assessment/sizing flows. Executed results are recorded separately in remediation evidence; this document does not mark the final audit resolved.
